1. Who we are

Gladiator Fit is the companion app for members of the Gladiator Fit gym (Tashkent, Uzbekistan). The app is developed and published by Ulug‘bek Qodirov (“we”). Contact: archduke.mobile.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • Sign-in data: your phone number and the name you enter. Gym reception confirms the sign-in request and links the app to your existing gym member record.
  • Gym member record (kept by the gym, shown to you in the app): name, phone, photo, membership, visits, payments and debts, trainer bookings, freeze requests, loyalty coins, referrals.
  • Fitness diary (synced to our server so it works on all your devices): meals and their calories/macros, favourite meals, water, weight log, fitness profile (sex, height, weight, goal, daily targets).
  • Health data from Health Connect (only if you allow it): daily step count and sleep duration. See section 4.
  • Photos and camera: the camera is used to scan the gym QR code, product barcodes and food photos. Food photos are kept only on your phone; for AI analysis the photo is sent to Google Gemini (section 5) and is not stored on our server.
  • AI coach messages: the questions you type and a short summary of today’s diary are sent to Google Gemini to generate an answer. Chat history is kept only on your phone.
  • AI reports: if you report an AI answer, we store the answer, your question, the reason and the app version.
  • Other: music votes in the gym, messages from reception, app settings and reminders (stored on your phone).

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How we use data

Only to provide the app’s features: sign-in, showing your membership and payments, gym check-in by QR, the food and activity diary, AI food analysis and coach answers, reminders, trainer bookings and music voting. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Health Connect

With your permission the app reads steps and sleep from Health Connect to fill in your daily activity in the diary. This data is stored with your diary on our server, shown only to you, and used only for the diary and AI coach tips you ask for. It is never sold, never used for advertising, credit or insurance decisions, and never shared with third parties except as needed to provide the feature. You can revoke the permission at any time in Health Connect settings. Our use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. AI features

Food photo analysis and the AI coach use Google Gemini through our own server, so the AI key is never stored in the app. Google processes the request under the Gemini API terms. AI answers may be inaccurate and are not medical advice. Every AI answer has a “Report” button.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase — database and server hosting.
  • Google Gemini API — AI food analysis and coach answers.
  • Open Food Facts — product lookup by barcode (only the barcode is sent).
  • YouTube — workout videos are played with the YouTube player (YouTube’s privacy policy applies).
  • Google Fonts — app fonts may be downloaded from Google servers.

Gym reception staff see the gym member record and sign-in requests. We may disclose data if required by law.

7. Security

Data is transferred over HTTPS. Database access goes through server functions that check your personal session token; other members cannot see your data.

8. Retention and deletion

App data is kept while you use the app. You can delete it at any time: Profile → “Delete account and data”, or see how to delete your account. Deletion removes the diary, weight, activity and health data, fitness profile, votes, messages, AI reports, sign-in requests and all sessions. Your gym member card, membership, payments and visit history are the gym’s accounting records kept by reception. To delete them too, tick “Also ask the gym to delete my member card” when deleting — the gym administrator receives the request — or contact the gym or email us.

9. Children

The app is intended for gym members and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data by emailing archduke.mobile.dev@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Разрешение можно отозвать в настройках Health Connect. Использование данных Health Connect соответствует политике разрешений Health Connect, включая требования Limited Use.

Данные приложения хранятся, пока вы им пользуетесь. Удалить их можно в любой момент: Профиль → «Удалить аккаунт и данные», или см. как удалить аккаунт. Чтобы удалить и их, при удалении отметьте «Также попросить зал удалить мою карточку» — администратор зала получит заявку, — или обратитесь в зал или напишите нам.
